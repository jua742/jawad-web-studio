01About the studio

07Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How does a project start?

Tell us about your project through the contact form. We will reply with questions and a custom quote. Work starts once the scope and the quote are agreed.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the scope. A landing page is quicker than a full business website or an online store. We give a timeline with the quote, before work starts.

How is the price decided?

Every project is quoted on its scope — the number of pages, the features needed and the design work involved. There are no fixed package prices. Use “Request a Custom Quote” and describe what you need.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business details, what the website should do, any content or images you already have, and examples of websites you like. If you do not have content yet, we can work from what you provide.

Do you look after the website after launch?

Yes. Website Maintenance covers updates, small changes and checks that the site stays working. Ask about it in the contact form.
